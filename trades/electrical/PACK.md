# Trade pack: electrical

Service, circuits, receptacle and lighting layout, GFCI and AFCI protection, smoke and CO alarm wiring.

**Status:** first version (framework build step 7).

Adds to a project:
- `.claude/skills/electrical-practice/SKILL.md`: how this trade's work is designed, checked, ordered and inspected.
- `docs/work/hints-electrical.md`: what a work order for this trade must specify, and which tasks an owner may do themselves.
- No checks of its own: smoke and CO alarms are building-code rules, checked by the shared `bldg_checks/irc.py` helpers.
