# Hints: foundation work

For the work-planner. What every foundation work order or owner instruction must say, beyond the templates.

## Work order (concrete or excavation contractor)
- **Drawings:** the foundation plan (S-0) and any details, at the issued-for-construction revision; footing sizes and depths below **finished** grade, and where the depth is measured from.
- **Concrete:** strength or bagged mix, air entrainment if the weathering region needs it, any reinforcement, the finish of exposed tops, curing and protection in cold or hot weather.
- **Anchors:** who sets post bases, anchor bolts or embedded hardware, and to what layout tolerance.
- **Hold point:** the footing inspection before concrete; the contractor must not pour until it passes. Who books it.
- **Utilities:** who calls 811 and keeps the ticket; hand-digging near marks.
- **Spoils and site:** where excavated soil goes, protection of lawn, trees, driveways; final grading away from the building.
- **Not included** (typical): framing, post installation, backfill around posts, landscaping repair.
- **Acceptance:** inspection passed; footing locations within the stated tolerance of the layout; tops at the stated elevation; anchors plumb and at layout.

## Owner instructions (e.g. deck piers with tube forms and bagged concrete)
- Before: permit posted; 811 marks in place and the ticket's start date reached; materials on site (tube forms, bagged mix, post bases, gravel if the design calls for it).
- Tools: layout string and stakes, tape, level, post-hole digger or rented auger (two-person auger for large holes), wheelbarrow and hoe or mixer, tamping bar.
- Steps with measurements: layout from the house or a fixed line, checked by diagonals (3-4-5 or equal diagonals) to the stated tolerance; hole diameter and **depth below finished grade, measured at every hole** (deeper is fine, shallower is not); bottom firm and undisturbed; tube form height and plumb.
- **Hold point:** stop for the footing inspection with holes open and forms in place. Record each hole's measured depth (M-x) in `docs/inspections.md`.
- Pour: mix per the bag, consolidate (rod it), set post bases at the layout point, check position and plumb before it sets; cure as the bag says (keep damp, protect from frost).
- Stop and call a professional if: water fills the holes, you hit rock, roots or fill you can't get through, you find any pipe, wire or marker tape, or the soil caves.
