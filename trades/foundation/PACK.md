# Trade pack: foundation

Footings, slabs, crawlspaces and basement walls; deck and shed footings; excavation and drainage.

**Status:** first version (framework build step 7).

- `model/bldg_checks/foundation.py`: `posts_on_footings` (every post wholly on a footing); tests in `model/tests/test_trade_foundation.py`.
