# Hints: framing work

For the work-planner. What every framing work order or owner instruction must say, beyond the templates.

## Work order (framing contractor)
- **Drawings:** framing plans and connection details at the issued-for-construction revision; every member's size, species and grade, spacing and span.
- **Products:** the connector and fastener schedule (maker, model, fastener type and count per the maker's sheet), corrosion rating for treated lumber; any engineered lumber with its maker's installation guide.
- **Lumber:** species and grade as in the takeoff, treatment for ground contact or above ground as specified; who supplies it.
- **Hold points:** footings passed before posts; the framing inspection before decking or covering; who books it.
- **Not included** (typical): footings, decking, guards and stairs (if separate), electrical, finish.
- **Acceptance:** inspection passed; members as drawn (size, spacing within the stated tolerance, crowns up), every hanger fully nailed per its sheet, ledger flashing as detailed, frame square (equal diagonals within the stated tolerance) and level.

## Owner instructions (e.g. a deck frame)
- Before: footing inspection passed and post bases set; lumber checked against the takeoff (grade stamps, treatment tags); connectors and the right fasteners on site.
- Tools: circular saw, drill/driver, framing square, 4 ft level, string line, tape, clamps, impact driver for structural screws or bolts as specified.
- Steps with measurements: ledger at the stated height and level, flashed, fastened in the stated pattern (spacing and edge distances as drawn); posts plumb and cut to height; beam crown up, level within the stated tolerance; joists at the stated spacing (each within ±1/4 in, never wider than stated), crown up, in hangers fully nailed; frame square by diagonals.
- **Hold point:** the framing inspection before decking, if required; record measured spacing and diagonals (M-x).
- Stop and call a professional if: the house rim behind the ledger is rotten, not solid, or not what `docs/existing.md` says; a member is cracked, badly twisted, or shorter than needed; a hanger doesn't fit the member; anything must be cut that the drawings don't show.
