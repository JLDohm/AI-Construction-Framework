# Trade pack: framing

Floor, wall and roof framing, headers, beams, decks, sheathing and connectors.

**Status:** first version (framework build step 7).

Adds to a project:
- `.claude/skills/framing-practice/SKILL.md`: how this trade's work is designed, checked, ordered and inspected.
- `docs/work/hints-framing.md`: what a work order for this trade must specify, and which tasks an owner may do themselves.
- `model/bldg_checks/framing.py`: `joists_supported` (every joist on two or more supports); tests in `model/tests/test_trade_framing.py`.
